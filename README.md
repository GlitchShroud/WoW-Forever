# WoW Forever — Warrior Talent Trees
A compact reference for the current Warrior talent-tree layout used by this calculator.

Summary of Changes:

Arms
- Impale now also grants +2 / +4% critical-strike chance.
- Improved Slam moved higher to Row 4.
- Improved Hamstring moved higher to Row 5.
- Bloodthrill reduced to 2 ranks with 5 / 10% proc chance and moved lower to Row 6.
- Two-Handed Weapon Specialization moved lower to Row 6 with +2 / 4 / 6% damage.

Fury
- Booming Voice reduced to 2 ranks with +25 / 50% radius.
- Unbridled Wrath now generates 2 additional Rage when procs, increased to 4 Rage with two-handed weapons.
- Improved Execute moved higher to Row 3.
- Improved Intercept moved higher to Row 4.
- Flurry swapped with Enrage and moved higher to Row 5.
- Raging Blows moved lower to Row 5.
- Enrage swapped with Flurry, moved lower to Row 6, reduced to 3 ranks, and now grants +5 / 10 / 15% Physical damage.
- Boundless Rage removed.

Protection
- Anticipation reduced to 3 ranks, granting +5 / 10 / 15 Defense Skill.
